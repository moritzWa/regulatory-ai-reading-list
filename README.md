# Regulatory AI Reading List

A curated reading list and toolset for teams applying AI to life-sciences regulatory affairs and quality - FDA warning letters, recalls, Complete Response Letters, 510(k) clearances, and UDI data.

Maintained by the team at [Arca](https://www.arca.inc) (we build an agentic platform for regulatory & quality teams). Suggestions welcome via issues or PRs.

## FDA guidance & primary sources

- FDA - [Artificial Intelligence & Medical Products](https://www.fda.gov/science-research/science-and-research-special-topics/artificial-intelligence-and-medical-products)
- FDA - [Artificial Intelligence-Enabled Device Software Functions: Lifecycle Management and Marketing Submissions (draft guidance)](https://www.fda.gov/regulatory-information/search-fda-guidance-documents/artificial-intelligence-enabled-device-software-functions-lifecycle-management-and-marketing)
- FDA - [Marketing Submission Recommendations for a Predetermined Change Control Plan for AI/ML-Enabled Device Software Functions](https://www.fda.gov/regulatory-information/search-fda-guidance-documents/marketing-submission-recommendations-predetermined-change-control-plan-artificial)
- FDA - [Good Machine Learning Practice for Medical Device Development: Guiding Principles](https://www.fda.gov/medical-devices/software-medical-device-samd/good-machine-learning-practice-medical-device-development-guiding-principles)
- FDA - [Data Dashboard](https://datadashboard.fda.gov/) - compliance actions, inspections, recalls, warning letters
- openFDA - [Device 510(k) endpoint](https://open.fda.gov/apis/device/510k/) and [device recall endpoint](https://open.fda.gov/apis/device/recall/)

## Analysis & commentary

- [FDA's First AI Warning Letter: The Model Wasn't the Problem](https://www.arca.inc/blog/fda-first-ai-warning-letter) - what the first AI-related FDA warning letter actually flagged (data integrity and process controls, not the model itself)
- FDA Digital Health Center of Excellence - [Digital Health Policy Navigator](https://www.fda.gov/medical-devices/digital-health-center-excellence/digital-health-policy-navigator)
- IMDRF - [Machine Learning-enabled Medical Devices: Key Terms and Definitions](https://www.imdrf.org/documents/machine-learning-enabled-medical-devices-key-terms-and-definitions)

## Standards & frameworks

- ISO 13485 - Quality management systems for medical devices
- ISO 14971 - Risk management for medical devices
- IEC 62304 - Medical device software life-cycle processes
- 21 CFR Part 11 - Electronic records and signatures
- 21 CFR Part 820 / QMSR - Quality system regulation (transitioning to QMSR, effective Feb 2026)
